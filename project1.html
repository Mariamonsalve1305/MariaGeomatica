Urban Mobility & Economic Indicators

This project analyzes the relationship between urban mobility and economic indicators across Latin America. By integrating multiple datasets and applying Python-based workflows, the analysis uncovers key spatial and economic dynamics.

Methodology

Data cleaning, spatial standardization, and correlation analysis were performed using Python, GeoPandas, and SQL.

Key Insights

Cities with optimized mobility patterns tend to show stronger economic performance, highlighting the importance of data-driven planning.

Tools Used

Python, Pandas, GeoPandas, SQL, Power BI
